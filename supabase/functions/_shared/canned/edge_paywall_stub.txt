Hims & Hers shares fall 18% after FDA narrows compounded GLP-1 carve-out

Telehealth company Hims & Hers Health Inc. saw shares fall 18% in pre-market trading after the U.S. Food and Drug Administration issued updated guidance narrowing the conditions under which compounding pharmacies may legally produce semaglutide and tirzepatide formulations.
